# Slime Breeder Mechanics

## Core Mechanics
- **Slime Breeding**: Breed slimes with unique traits.
- **Evolution System**: Evolve slimes for combat.
- **Battle System**: Fight enemies with slimes.

## Progression System
- **Slime Upgrades**: Unlock new abilities and traits.
- **Combat Efficiency**: Improve battle performance.

## Win/Loss Conditions
- **Win**: Defeat the final boss.
- **Loss**: All slimes die.
