# Dungeon Crawler Mechanics

## Core Mechanics
- **Procedural Generation**: Explore randomly generated dungeons.
- **Loot System**: Collect gear, weapons, and items.
- **Combat System**: Fight enemies and bosses.

## Progression System
- **Character Upgrades**: Improve stats and abilities.
- **Dungeon Depth**: Explore deeper levels for rarer loot.

## Win/Loss Conditions
- **Win**: Defeat the final boss.
- **Loss**: Player death.
