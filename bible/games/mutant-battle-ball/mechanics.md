# Mutant Battle Ball Mechanics

## Core Mechanics
- **Team-Based Combat**: Multiplayer battles with customizable teams.
- **Upgrade System**: Improve character stats and abilities.
- **Sports Arenas**: Dynamic environments for combat.

## Progression System
- **Character Upgrades**: Unlock new abilities and equipment.
- **Arena Unlocks**: Access new arenas with unique challenges.

## Win/Loss Conditions
- **Win**: Defeat all enemies.
- **Loss**: Team elimination.
