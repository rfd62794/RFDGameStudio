# SlimeWorld Mechanics

## Core Mechanics
- **Exploration**: Discover new areas and secrets.
- **Slime Collection**: Collect slimes to upgrade abilities.
- **Combat**: Fight enemies and bosses.
- **Progression**: Unlock new abilities and areas.

## Progression System
- **Ability Upgrades**: Unlock new slime-based abilities.
- **Area Unlocks**: Explore new areas to find rare slimes.

## Win/Loss Conditions
- **Win**: Defeat the final boss.
- **Loss**: Player death.
