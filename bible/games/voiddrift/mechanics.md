# VoidRift Mechanics

## Core Mechanics
- **Resource Scavenging**: Collect materials to upgrade the drone.
- **Drone Upgrades**: Improve speed, health, and weapons.
- **Procedural Exploration**: Discover new areas and secrets.
- **Survival Hazards**: Avoid environmental dangers (e.g., radiation, void storms).
- **Enemy Encounters**: Fight or evade hostile entities.

## Progression System
- **Drone Upgrades**: Unlock new abilities and equipment.
- **Exploration Depth**: Deeper areas contain rarer resources and enemies.

## Win/Loss Conditions
- **Win**: Reach the Core of the Void and escape with the artifact.
- **Loss**: Drone destruction (0 health) or oxygen depletion.
