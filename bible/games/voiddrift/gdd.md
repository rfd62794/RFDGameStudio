# VoidRift Game Design Document

## Core Loop
Scavenge resources → Upgrade drone → Explore deeper → Survive hazards.

## Win Condition
Reach the **Core of the Void** and escape with the artifact.

## Loss Condition
Drone destruction (0 health remaining) or oxygen depletion.

## Mechanics
- **Resource Scavenging**: Collect materials to upgrade the drone.
- **Drone Upgrades**: Improve speed, health, and weapons.
- **Procedural Exploration**: Discover new areas and secrets.
- **Survival Hazards**: Avoid environmental dangers (e.g., radiation, void storms).
- **Enemy Encounters**: Fight or evade hostile entities.

## Theme
Sci-fi survival and exploration in a procedurally generated void.
