# Rogue Slither Mechanics

## Core Mechanics
- **Procedural Evolution**: Evolve your snake with unique traits.
- **Roguelike Progression**: Explore new areas and unlock abilities.
- **Boss Fights**: Defeat bosses to progress.

## Progression System
- **Ability Upgrades**: Unlock new snake abilities.
- **Area Unlocks**: Explore new areas to find rare items.

## Win/Loss Conditions
- **Win**: Defeat the final boss.
- **Loss**: Player death.
