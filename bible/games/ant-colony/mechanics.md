# Ant Colony Mechanics

## Core Mechanics
- **Colony Expansion**: Build and expand the colony.
- **Resource Management**: Collect and manage resources.
- **Defense System**: Protect the colony from threats.

## Progression System
- **Colony Upgrades**: Unlock new structures and abilities.
- **Resource Efficiency**: Improve collection and storage.

## Win/Loss Conditions
- **Win**: Survive 30 days.
- **Loss**: Colony destruction.
