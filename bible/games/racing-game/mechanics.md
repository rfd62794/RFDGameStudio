# Racing Game Mechanics

## Core Mechanics
- **Speed Mechanics**: Accelerate, brake, and drift.
- **Upgrades**: Improve vehicle performance.
- **Competition**: Race against opponents.

## Progression System
- **Vehicle Customization**: Upgrade and customize vehicles.
- **Track Unlocks**: Unlock new tracks and challenges.

## Win/Loss Conditions
- **Win**: Win 10 races.
- **Loss**: Last place.
