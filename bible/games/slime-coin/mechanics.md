# Slime Coin Mechanics

## Core Mechanics
- **Resource Mining**: Collect resources to trade and upgrade.
- **Economic System**: Buy/sell resources and upgrades.
- **Bankruptcy Risk**: Manage finances to avoid loss.

## Progression System
- **Resource Upgrades**: Improve mining efficiency and capacity.
- **Market Expansion**: Unlock new trading opportunities.

## Win/Loss Conditions
- **Win**: Collect 1M coins.
- **Loss**: Bankruptcy.
