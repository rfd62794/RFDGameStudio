# Shoal Mechanics

## Core Mechanics
- **Fish Breeding**: Breed and raise fish with unique traits.
- **Aquarium Management**: Maintain water quality and tank conditions.
- **Market Economy**: Sell fish for profit and expand the aquarium.

## Progression System
- **Fish Genetics**: Breed fish with desirable traits.
- **Tank Expansion**: Upgrade tank size and conditions.

## Win/Loss Conditions
- **Win**: Build a thriving aquarium.
- **Loss**: All fish die.
