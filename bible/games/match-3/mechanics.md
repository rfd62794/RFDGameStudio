# Match 3 Mechanics

## Core Mechanics
- **Tile Swapping**: Swap adjacent tiles to create matches.
- **Matching**: Clear tiles by matching 3 or more.
- **Level Progression**: Complete levels to advance.

## Progression System
- **Power-Ups**: Unlock special abilities.
- **Level Design**: Solve increasingly complex puzzles.

## Win/Loss Conditions
- **Win**: Clear 100 levels.
- **Loss**: Game over.
