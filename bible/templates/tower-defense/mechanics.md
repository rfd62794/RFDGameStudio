# Tower Defense Mechanics

## Core Mechanics
- **Tower Building**: Build and upgrade towers.
- **Wave Survival**: Survive increasingly difficult waves.
- **Base Defense**: Protect the base from enemies.

## Progression System
- **Tower Upgrades**: Improve tower damage and range.
- **Wave Design**: Survive more challenging waves.

## Win/Loss Conditions
- **Win**: Survive 20 waves.
- **Loss**: Base destruction.
