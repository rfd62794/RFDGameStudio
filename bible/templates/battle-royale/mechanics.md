# Battle Royale Mechanics

## Core Mechanics
- **Loot System**: Collect weapons, armor, and items.
- **Elimination Zones**: Survive shrinking play zones.
- **Last-Man-Standing Combat**: Eliminate opponents to win.

## Progression System
- **Loadout Upgrades**: Improve gear and abilities.
- **Dynamic Zones**: Adapt to changing environments.

## Win/Loss Conditions
- **Win**: Be the last player standing.
- **Loss**: Player death.
