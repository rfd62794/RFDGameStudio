# Tycoon Game Mechanics

## Core Mechanics
- **Business Management**: Build and manage businesses.
- **Resource Allocation**: Allocate resources for growth.
- **Expansion**: Expand operations and revenue.

## Progression System
- **Business Upgrades**: Improve efficiency and profitability.
- **Market Expansion**: Enter new markets and industries.

## Win/Loss Conditions
- **Win**: Reach 1B net worth.
- **Loss**: Bankruptcy.
