# Zombie Survival Mechanics

## Core Mechanics
- **Scavenging System**: Collect resources to survive.
- **Base Building**: Build and upgrade defenses.
- **Survival System**: Manage health, hunger, and thirst.

## Progression System
- **Base Upgrades**: Improve defenses and resources.
- **Survival Efficiency**: Extend survival duration.

## Win/Loss Conditions
- **Win**: Survive 100 days.
- **Loss**: Player death.
