# Strategy Game Mechanics

## Core Mechanics
- **Planning**: Develop strategies and tactics.
- **Conquest**: Expand territory and defeat opponents.
- **Resource Management**: Collect and manage resources.

## Progression System
- **Tech Tree**: Unlock new technologies and units.
- **Territory Expansion**: Control more of the map.

## Win/Loss Conditions
- **Win**: Control 50% of the map.
- **Loss**: Defeat.
