# RPG Game Mechanics

## Core Mechanics
- **Exploration**: Discover new areas and secrets.
- **Combat**: Fight enemies and bosses.
- **Leveling**: Gain experience and improve stats.

## Progression System
- **Quest System**: Complete quests to advance the story.
- **Gear Upgrades**: Improve weapons and armor.

## Win/Loss Conditions
- **Win**: Defeat the final boss.
- **Loss**: Player death.
