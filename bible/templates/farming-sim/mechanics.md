# Farming Sim Mechanics

## Core Mechanics
- **Crop Planting**: Plant and harvest crops.
- **Animal Husbandry**: Raise and breed animals.
- **Market Economy**: Sell produce for profit.

## Progression System
- **Farm Upgrades**: Improve tools, land, and storage.
- **Economic Growth**: Expand the farm and business.

## Win/Loss Conditions
- **Win**: Earn 1M coins.
- **Loss**: Bankruptcy.
