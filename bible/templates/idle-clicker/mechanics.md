# Idle Clicker Mechanics

- **Click Mechanics**: Click to earn resources.
- **Automation**: Upgrade to automate resource collection.
- **Progression**: Unlock new upgrades and milestones.

## Progression System
- **Upgrade Path**: Improve click efficiency and automation.
- **Milestone Unlocks**: Reach new goals and rewards.

- **Loss**: Stagnation.
