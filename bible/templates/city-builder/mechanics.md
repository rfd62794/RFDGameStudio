# City Builder Mechanics

## Core Mechanics
- **Zoning System**: Designate residential, commercial, and industrial zones.
- **Resource Management**: Collect taxes and manage resources.
- **Urban Expansion**: Expand the city and unlock new areas.

## Progression System
- **Infrastructure Upgrades**: Improve roads, utilities, and services.
- **Population Growth**: Attract more residents and businesses.

## Win/Loss Conditions
- **Win**: Reach 1M population.
- **Loss**: Bankruptcy.
