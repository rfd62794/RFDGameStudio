# Drone Defense Mechanics

## Core Mechanics
- **Tower Defense**: Build and upgrade drones to defend against waves.
- **Wave System**: Survive increasingly difficult enemy waves.
- **Base Upgrades**: Improve base defenses and resources.

## Progression System
- **Drone Upgrades**: Unlock new drone types and abilities.
- **Base Expansion**: Upgrade base defenses and resources.

## Win/Loss Conditions
- **Win**: Survive 10 waves.
- **Loss**: Base destruction.
