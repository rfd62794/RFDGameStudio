# Puzzle Game Mechanics

## Core Mechanics
- **Logic-Based Puzzles**: Solve puzzles using logic and strategy.
- **Progression**: Advance through increasingly difficult puzzles.
- **Mastery**: Achieve high scores and completion times.

## Progression System
- **Puzzle Design**: Solve a variety of puzzle types.
- **Score System**: Earn points for efficiency and speed.

## Win/Loss Conditions
- **Win**: Complete all puzzles.
- **Loss**: Stagnation.
