# Platformer Mechanics

## Core Mechanics
- **Running**: Move the character left and right.
- **Jumping**: Navigate obstacles and platforms.
- **Collecting**: Gather items and power-ups.

## Progression System
- **Level Progression**: Complete levels to unlock new areas.
- **Ability Upgrades**: Unlock new movement and combat abilities.

## Win/Loss Conditions
- **Win**: Reach the end of the game.
- **Loss**: Player death.
