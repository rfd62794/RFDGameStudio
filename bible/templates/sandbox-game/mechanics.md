# Sandbox Game Mechanics

## Core Mechanics
- **Building**: Construct structures and environments.
- **Exploration**: Discover new areas and resources.
- **Creativity**: Design and create unique content.

## Progression System
- **Challenge System**: Complete challenges to unlock rewards.
- **Resource Management**: Collect and manage resources.

## Win/Loss Conditions
- **Win**: Complete 100 challenges.
- **Loss**: Stagnation.
