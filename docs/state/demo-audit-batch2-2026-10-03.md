## Findings worth acting on
1. planetforge is registered `external` with embedUrl `/arcade/planetforge/` but is not deployed and is absent from the live /games/ index (404 on both paths).
2. slimeworld (stable) renders the SLIMEGARDEN "MVP // PHASE_E" lab terminal inside its iframe, identical to the slimegarden Origin page. The stable demo may be serving the origin build; verify.
3. slimegarden and slimeworld overflow horizontally at 390 px (iframe scrollWidth 476 vs 368); trinity_siege slightly (370 vs 358).
4. 7 of 15 show no restart/new-game label in the game: shoal, slimebreeder, slither_rogue, succession, systemic_extract, voiddrift_redux, wire_rust (planetforge not testable).
5. trinity_siege's public blurb contains an internal note ("LEAST-VERIFIED: ... fabricated combat logic").
6. Slugs are hyphenated on the site, but iframe paths for slime_coin, systemic_extract, trinity_siege use underscores; the spec's `/games/<id>/` pattern with underscore ids would 404.
