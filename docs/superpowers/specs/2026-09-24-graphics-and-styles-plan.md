## 3. Where graphics stand today

Measured on main 2026-09-24:

- **SVG is the default.** `engine/artGen` (seeded `mulberry32`, 11 icon shapes, polygons, the
  glow filter). Paper Doll's production renderer (hand-authored React SVG, 4 body types, socketed
  on a 400x480 viewBox, 4 facings). SlimeVisual, RoleSymbol, SVGRacer, StickFighter, PlanetMap.
  106 generated Dissonance SVG files.
- **Canvas 2D in five games:** shoal, slime_coin, slither_rogue, voiddrift_redux, and
  mutant_battle_ball (a canvas court with Paper Doll SVG over it).
- **DOM and CSS for the rest.** Brewfield, choke_point, scrapcrawl, succession,
  house_of_kings_collab, wire_rust, early_learning_buddy. Motion comes from `framer-motion` /
  `motion`, and gladiator_arena uses `canvas-confetti`.
- **No WebGL, no raster pipeline.** One PNG in `ts/` (the `wolf.png` fixture). No sprite sheet,
  atlas, Aseprite loader, `image-rendering: pixelated` or `imageSmoothingEnabled`. No graphics
  engine dependency (no pixi, three, konva or regl).
- **Palettes:** `ui/tokens.css` variables; Paper Doll's `resolveColor` hierarchy and 6 brand
  palettes; the glossary's tone roles (`ember`, `spark`, `ash`, `cinder`, `danger`, `heal`,
  `neutral`, `gold`) with no resolver yet.
- **Fonts:** none bundled (Google Fonts by `@import`). A pixel font, `PublicPixel`, sits in
  `archive/rpgCore/assets/shared/fonts/` with no licence file.
- **Tests:** byte-identical checks on the Dissonance SVGs, plus seed and geometry tests. No
  golden snapshots of creatures and no screenshot tests.

## 4. The model: one definition, many styles

The rule that joins every thread: **art is defined once and drawn in a style.** A style is a
rendering choice, never a second art set. ADR-006's economics depend on this: creature content
is the budget constraint, and parallel sets would double it.

**Art sources.** Each thing on screen comes from exactly one of these:
- **Procedural vector:** `artGen`, the Paper Doll composer, SlimeVisual geometry. Seeded, so the
  same seed always gives the same shape.
- **Hand-authored vector:** Paper Doll Studio brand parts, RoleSymbol.
- **Generated static vector:** the Dissonance generator, which writes files at build time.
- **Pre-rendered raster:** `creatureArt` (anyCreature 3D output), and painted backdrops when they
  arrive.
- **Per-frame canvas:** Shoal, Slither Rogue, VoidDrift and similar.

**Styles.** Each style is a way of drawing those sources:
- **`vector`**, the default: full palette, gradients, smooth easing.
- **`pixel`**: rasterise at 32 or 48 px, quantise to the style palette, outline, integer upscale,
  pixel font, stepped easing.
- **`shaded`**: a style parameter rather than a separate style. It applies to vector (and pixel)
  once the shading port lands. The technique comparison's flat-vs-shaded check says flat colour
  hurts every technique.
- **Themes (tone palettes):** `dark`, `light`, per-game palettes and colour-blind-safe sets. They
  recolour through tone roles, never baked hex.

**Sprite sheets are an output, not a source.** A seeded export draws any vector source (in any
style) into frames and writes `sheet.png` plus Aseprite-compatible JSON. The same seed always
produces the same sheet. The same small loader then plays any sheet, whether exported here or
drawn by hand in Aseprite later.

**What each style applies to:**

| Source | vector | pixel | shaded |
|---|---|---|---|
| Procedural and hand-authored vector | as authored | rasterise → quantise → outline | shade factor on fills |
| Generated static vector (Dissonance) | tone-resolved inline SVG | same pixel path | same |
| Pre-rendered raster (`creatureArt`) | as authored | quantise to the palette (optional) | n/a |
| Per-frame canvas scenes | as authored | overlay and creatures only; the scene stays as authored (foundation spec §8) | n/a |

## 5. Decisions only Robert makes (they gate phase 2 onward)

- **D1. Technique winners** from the 10-technique comparison (`localhost:5200`). Which of the
  smooth family (Bézier, metaball, stroke, noise, superellipse, SDF, canvas) become artGen
  primitives? Is the 32x32 pixel grid (technique 9) a *style* (this plan's reading) or a separate
  art direction?
- **D2. The Bézier primitive:** keep it and build it into artGen, or drop it (`localhost:5199`).
- **D3. Shading:** port ChimeraLab's shade factor and edge light as the `shaded` parameter, yes or
  no.
- **D4. The pixel look:** which 16-colour palette (a named public palette, or one derived from
  existing brand colours), which target size (32 or 48 px), and the pixel font. `PublicPixel`'s
  licence must be confirmed before it is bundled; otherwise use an OFL-licensed pixel font.
- **D5. First adopters:** the foundation spec says Dissonance in both styles, plus Chimera Wilds
  *or* Mutant Battle Ball. Pick one.

Until D1-D3 are made, phases 0-2 proceed. They are neutral to the outcome.

## 6. Phased plan

Each phase merges on its own and leaves non-adopting games unchanged, as in foundation spec §9.
Directives are written per step when it comes up. Steps carry their roadmap ids.

**Phase 0: decide and document**
- Robert's decisions D1-D5 (the prose roadmap's Now).
- **M6.1: `docs/GRAPHICS.md`** covers:
  - the source/style model of §4 and the vector / raster / canvas rule;
  - a file-size and load budget per side, measured against the portal targets in
    `RFDGameStudio_MultiPortalPublishing.md`;
  - which game uses which tech, from §3.

**Phase 1: foundation layers (the dropped milestone, added as M8)**
- **M8.1-M8.3: foundation steps 2-4.** The overlay (anchors, chips, tooltips, DetailCard, event
  log), the `diff` and scheduler juice with its primitives and presets, then events through the
  bridge. Dissonance is the first adopter. The pixel overlay (phase 3) styles this layer, so it
  comes first.

**Phase 2: determinism and tokens**
- **M5.1-M5.2** (existing): `CREATURE_SYSTEM.md` and golden SVG snapshots for fixed seeds.
  Rasterised output is only testable once the SVG it comes from is pinned.
- **M6.4: the tone resolver.** Resolve glossary tone roles to colours per style and theme,
  starting in `ts/src/foundation/style/`. Replace hard-coded `ELEMENT_COLORS` in both Dissonance
  generators with tone roles. The byte-identical baseline is re-blessed in the same commit.

**Phase 3: the pixel style**
- **M6.5: a deterministic rasteriser.** `svgToCanvas` (already in `artGen/shapes.ts`) is extended
  to rasterise at a target size, quantise to the style palette, run an outline pass and upscale
  by whole multiples. Golden PNG hashes for fixed seeds, next to M5.2's SVG goldens.
- **M6.3: pixel style as a renderer option** (existing step, extended). `ui.yaml` `style:` and
  `style_toggle:`, the choice remembered per game (with a wrapped storage fallback), the pixel
  overlay look (font, 1px outlines, no blur, `image-rendering: pixelated`, stepped easing), and
  creatures through M6.5. Adopters: Dissonance, plus the D5 creature game. This is foundation
  step 5.

**Phase 4: sprite sheets**
- **M6.2: vector-to-sprite-sheet export** (existing step, extended). Seeded frames for a pose or
  animation (`calculatePose` for Paper Doll) in any style. Writes `sheet.png` plus
  Aseprite-compatible JSON (frames, durations, tags).
- **M6.6: the sprite-sheet loader and player.** A small loader for the Aseprite JSON format, plus
  a `<Sprite>` / canvas `drawSprite` player. It closes the tooling catalog's gap and plays both
  exported and hand-drawn sheets.
- **M6.7: adopters.**
  - Shoal's `drawFish` / `drawSharksBatched` consume generated sprites.
  - SlimeVisual uses the shared seeded RNG and polygon utilities.
  - `creatureArt` gets its first real consumer, uses `fallbackPathFor` to fall back to a
    generated sprite, and gets optional pixel quantisation.

**Phase 5: shading and styling (after D3)**
- **M6.8: `shaded` as a style parameter.** Port the ChimeraLab shade factor and edge light into
  artGen and Paper Doll fills, then re-bless the goldens.
- **The Brand / Cyber-Organic / Quality styling system** (prose Next, Paper Doll): once its
  Design.md exists, it builds on tone roles and the shading parameter rather than on new palettes.

**Later (prose roadmap Later)**
- Theme sets beyond vector and pixel: light and dark, colour-blind-safe palettes.
- Painted raster backdrops under the budget.
- Dissonance art on its remaining screens, with hover and animation on the art.
- Paper Doll animation beyond poses: sheet-driven idle and attack loops.
- Posture blending and normalised socket coordinates (composition, not style; listed here for
  their rendering effect).
- AntSim Redux visual feedback lives in the external antsim_redux repo (an embed here); it can
  reuse the juice primitives once M8 lands.

## 8. Out of scope

- 3D rendering, WebGL, and full 2D engines. The tooling catalog records no shader need (regl/ogl).
- Redrawing a canvas game's own scene art in pixel style (foundation spec §8).
- Model-driven art generation: the `tools/svg-generator`, `pixel-art-converter` and `3d-modeler`
  stubs. Asset generation stays seeded and deterministic here, and anyCreature stays its own
  pipeline behind the `creatureArt` seam.
- Hand-authored replacement art. That is content, and it arrives through the same sources.

## 9. Testing, as a rule for every step

- Seeded in, byte-identical out: SVG goldens (M5.2), PNG hash goldens (M6.5), sheet goldens
  (M6.2).
- Both styles render for every adopting game. Playwright captures one screenshot per style per
  adopter. It is a smoke check, not a pixel diff.
- Budgets from `docs/GRAPHICS.md` are checked by a test against the built asset sizes.
- `prefers-reduced-motion` and the stepped pixel easing are covered by the scheduler tests
  (foundation spec §10).
