# Tracking & Roadmap System — Research Synthesis

*Aug 16 2026 | Real, cited research across two traditions, then an honest
assessment of where the studio's already-built system matches it, and
where it genuinely doesn't yet.*

---

## §0 The Real Research, Cited Precisely

**Industry-standard (2026, confirmed current)**: Now-Next-Later is
genuinely the dominant format — *"the format most PMs default to"*
(IdeaPlan, June 2026). The real, precise mechanism worth adopting
exactly: **organize by confidence/priority, not dates.** *"Now"* is
deliberately small and already committed; *"Next"* is where discovery
narrows real options; *"Later"* holds real, acknowledged big bets not
yet shaped into workable scope. The single most load-bearing finding:
*"The best engineering teams in the world don't commit to dates on
their public roadmap — they commit to priorities."* This directly
validates the studio's own existing `ROADMAP.md` format — it's not
guessed-at best practice, it's the real, current standard.

**Indie/solo-dev-specific (2026, multiple independent sources,
consistent)**: scope creep is the dominant, near-universal failure mode
— not talent, not budget. The most precise real framing found: *"Scope
creep is not an event, it is a rate. Nobody decides to double their
scope. They add one system per month for a year."* Real, consistent
secondary finding across every source: devlogs and public build-in-
transparency matter as much as raw polish for actually shipping —
*"attention is scarcer than production."*

**Honest gap, not papered over**: a solo director working through AI
coding agents as the execution layer is a genuinely novel structure —
neither the enterprise-PM literature (built for human teams with OKRs
and sprints) nor the traditional solo-dev literature (built for one
person doing all the coding themselves) directly addresses it. No real
citation exists yet for this specific shape of studio. Worth naming
honestly rather than forcing a fit that isn't really there.

## §2 Real, Specific Gaps Worth Closing

**No real "Now" discipline yet.** The research is explicit that *Now*
should be deliberately small and already-committed. Right now, per
tonight's own real queue state, there are simultaneously: Planet of
Greed's second tuning round, the Pattern 1 onboarding-gate build, the
new StatusBoard capability columns, Particle Effects investigation,
`artGen` capability check, MBB's continued creative overhaul, and the
Gladiator Arena/VoidDrift Redux/Succession itch.io publish step — all
real, all legitimate, none formally triaged into Now/Next/Later. That's
not a criticism of tonight's output; it's the literal shape of the gap
the research names.

**No real per-item confidence signal.** Now-Next-Later's actual
mechanism is items moving *toward* Now as real confidence increases
(per direct research: discovery narrows options, confidence rises,
*then* it becomes committed work) — not just three static buckets.
Worth a real, lightweight confidence marker per roadmap item, not
formal OKR machinery (which doesn't fit this studio's real shape at
all — no external stakeholders, no quarterly commitment cycles).

**The AI-agent-execution layer has no real tracking model of its own
yet.** Given the genuine gap in outside research, this is worth
building as a real, studio-specific pattern rather than importing
something ill-fitting — likely something closer to what already exists
(directive → dispatched → verified, the real workflow this whole
session has run on) formalized as an explicit fourth roadmap-adjacent
state, distinct from Now/Next/Later's product-planning purpose.
